## Assessment

### Pre-requisite - Fixed Heating

It must be demonstrated that the main living space has an adequately sized fixed heating system. The main living space is the largest room that is used for general, everyday living – for example a lounge or family room. If this is open plan (such as including a kitchen or dining room), calculations must include all of the adjoining spaces if they are not able to be physically closed off. This includes any open stairwells connected to this space, as well as any other rooms open to the stairwell that cannot be closed off.

Heater(s) must be fixed (not portable), at least 1.5kW in heating capacity and meet the minimum required heating capacity for the main living room. The capacity required can be calculated using the [Tenancy Services Heating Assessment Tool](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/heating-tool/) (for homes compliant with 2008 insulation values, or apartments) or ECCHO.

In most cases, the acceptable types of heater(s) will be a larger fixed heating device like a heat pump, wood burner, pellet burner or flued gas heater. In some cases, such as small apartments, a fixed electric heater may be enough.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Note that for any Homestar rating all combustion appliances must be room-sealed - see credits [HC3 ](hc3-ventilation.md)and [HC4](hc4-moisture-control.md)
{% endhint %}

Unacceptable heaters include:

* open fire
* un-flued combustion heater
* heat pumps or electric heaters without a thermostat
* electric heaters when the required heating capacity is more than 2.4kW

### Heating Demand Compliance

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Calculation (ECCHO)" %}
Using the plans and specifications for the dwelling, up to 22 points may be awarded by completing a heating demand calculation using the Homestar Energy and Carbon Calculator for Homes (ECCHO) assuming a heating temperature of 20°C 24/7. Points are awarded as shown in the Credit Criteria.

For instructions on using ECCHO please refer to Appendix B.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Energy Modelling" %}
Using the plans and specifications for the dwelling, estimate the space heating demand for the home using an approved energy modelling software package and by following the Homestar Energy Modelling Protocol (see Appendix A).
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Passive House certification" %}
Points are awarded as shown in the Credit Criteria. Points are calculated by entering the estimated heating load from the energy modelling software into the ECCHO.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
