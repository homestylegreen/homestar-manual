# LV1: Inclusive Design

## Aim

_To encourage and recognise dwellings that are inclusive, visitable, easily adaptable, and accessible, to meet the changing needs of current and future occupants._

## Credit Summary

| Points Available   | 3    |
| ------------------ | ---- |
| Mandatory Minimums | None |
| Project-wide       | No   |

## Credit Criteria

Points are awarded based on the specific features in the dwelling, using a checklist, or through a third-party assessment, such as Lifemark™ Certification.

## Assessment

### Checklist Pathway

To achieve points, the design must include all the features on the relevant checklist.

<details>

<summary><strong>Design for Vision Impaired Occupants Checklist</strong></summary>

Achieve all requirements below:

**Glare control**

* [ ] Matte colours used for walls, doors and floor.
* [ ] Floor surfaces have a matte finish.
* [ ] Lighting is diffused to avoid a reflective effect on floors below ceiling mounted lights.

**Depth perception**

* [ ] Skirtings more than 100mm high are the same colour as the walls.

**Hazard mitigation**

* [ ] Stair edgings, handrails and other safety features are detailed and coloured to stand out from the background.

**Colour contrast**

* [ ] Light Reflectance Value (LRV) of adjacent walls and ceiling is at least 30 points apart.
* [ ] Doors and door handles, switches, and power/phone outlets contrast with the background.

**Switch and power point placement**

* [ ] All light switches, service controls and door handles are aligned and located between 900mm and 1200mm above finished floor level.
* [ ] Each room has at least one power outlet between 900mm and 1200mm above finished floor level.
* [ ] Where applicable, a phone jack point (BS6312, 6P2C or 8P8C) is located between 900mm and 1200mm above finished floor level.
* [ ] Switches, power point and phone jack points are placed in a cluster within 300mm of a doorway (measured from the edge of the opening).

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Visitable Design Checklist</strong></summary>

Achieve all requirements below:

**Entrance and internal access**

* [ ] Step-free, slip-resistant path that extends to property boundary and any vehicle parking area within the boundary has a gradient of no more than 1 in 12 and width of at least 1200mm.
* [ ] Additional for apartment buildings: the path from the building entrance to the dwelling entrance is slip-resistant with step-free access.
* [ ] Hallways are at least 1050mm wide.
* [ ] All internal and external doors have a minimum clear opening of 810mm (860mm door leaf).
* [ ] The entry is under cover and slip resistant.

**Habitable rooms**

* [ ] There is a habitable room on the entry level of the house.

**Bathroom**

* [ ] A toilet is located on the entry level.
* [ ] There is provision for a future clear transfer space of 800mm beside and/or in front of the toilet. The toilet pan is 450-460mm from the side wall.
* [ ] The vanity extends a minimum of 400mm from the back wall.
* [ ] The room is step-free.

**Fixtures and fittings**

* [ ] There are lever handles on all doors.
* [ ] Power points, TV, phone and computer outlets are a minimum of 300mm above the finished floor.
* [ ] Light switches, other service controls and door handles are aligned at between 900mm to 1200mm above the finished floor level.

**Kitchen**

* [ ] Dining and cooking areas are located next to each other.

**Car parking**

* [ ] Designated disable parking or a safe drop off zone with step -free access to the property. The safe drop-off zone has space for a vehicle to park to pick up and drop off a disabled rider, with step-free access to a footpath and without requiring the rider to move through moving or parked traffic.

OR

* [ ] Vehicle parking is provided with space adjacent to the parking spot that can be widened to 3500mm.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Adaptable Design Checklist</strong></summary>

Achieve all requirements below:

**Visitable**

* [ ] All requirements met from Visitable Design Checklist.

**Kitchen**

* [ ] All appliances are located at least 300mm from a corner.
* [ ] The minimum distance between two fixed kitchen benches is 1200mm (e.g. kitchen island and wall-fixed bench).

**Bedroom**

* [ ] There is space on the entry living level where a standard single bed (measuring 900mm x 1900mm) can fit.
* [ ] There is a minimum 800mm clear space available around one side and the foot of the bed.
* [ ] There is a minimum 800mm wide path from the door to the side of the bed.
* [ ] This room is not the main living area.

**Bathroom**

* [ ] The bathroom walls are reinforced to provide a fixing surface for grab rails.
* [ ] There is, or there is provision for (including plumbing), an entry level shower with a minimum dimension of 1200mm x 1200mm.

**Laundry**

* [ ] The laundry space or room is large enough to provide at least 1050mm clearance in front of fixed benches and appliances.

**Stairs (multi-storey dwellings)**

* [ ] Stairwells have a minimum clear width of 1000mm and are straight in design with no winders.
* [ ] A handrail is installed along the entirety of the staircase.

</details>

<summary><strong>Other</strong></summary>

The project has completed all of the following:

* [ ] Worked with the intended occupants to identify their specific, contextual needs, as well as those of others associated with the dwelling (residents and visitors, including service providers and emergency services).
* [ ] Consulted a practitioner or advocacy organisation with expertise in those specific accessibility needs in order to establish how to best meet these needs.
* [ ] Detailed report as to how these recommendations were implemented in the project.
