# Glossary

## Adhesives and sealants

Any adhesive and sealant product used in an interior application (including both exposed and concealed applications) and applied onsite.

## Air changes

The measure of air volume added to or removed from a space.

## Air permeability

Measure of inward or outward air leakage through the building envelope.

## Apartment

Self-contained dwelling that only occupies part of a building.

## Applied coating

Any liquid-applied finish including paints, varnish, stains, and oils used in either interior or exterior application.

## Average daylight factor (ADF)

Average of the ratio of the light level inside a structure to the light level outside the structure.

## Brownfield development

A property development which previously contained at least one building with permanent foundations within the previous legal boundary and in which the number of dwellings is no less than the number on-site prior to development, and any previously cultivated or pastoral farmland contains no more than 50% of the total footprint.

## Building footprint (BF)

The area of land that is taken up by the permanent foundations of the dwelling(s). Refer to Terminology for more details.

## Built Rating

Homestar rating of a completed dwelling; a physical inspection of the property by the Homestar Assessor is required.

## CIBSE

Chartered Institution of Building Services Engineers.

## Climate zone

Designation of areas within New Zealand that share similar climatic characteristics.

## Competent professional

Individual possessing the specialised knowledge, technical aptitude, and experience required to carry out the required task to an acceptable standard. This includes having a formal qualification (or minimum one year of experience) and a professional role relating to the field in question.

## Conditioned floor area (CFA)

Space within the thermal envelope of the dwelling that could maintain a temperature band of between 20–25°C for 365 days of the year. Refer to Terminology for more details.

## Continuous extract ventilation

Whole-dwelling ventilation system that extracts air continuously at a low rate.

## Design Rating

Homestar rating of a proposed dwelling based on detailed plans and specifications; expires two years after issue.

## Drip line

The edge of a specimen or area of land located directly under the outer circumference of a shrub or tree’s branches, used to ascertain the percentage of site covered by native species.

## EC

Electronically commutated; electric motors with permanent magnets on the rotor and electronics to control the voltage and current applied to the motor.

## Floor coverings

Coverings installed over the wooden or concrete floor structure, including but not limited to carpet, parquet, wood planks, laminate, tiles, and linoleum.

## fRsi

Temperature factor; difference between the interior surface temperature and the exterior air temperature, divided by the average temperature difference between interior and exterior.

## G-value

A measure of how much solar heat (infrared radiation) is allowed through a glazing unit relative to no glass.

## Gross floor area (GFA)

Building Footprint (BF) plus all areas of fully enclosed parts of the building built on top of the BF, including area taken up by internal and external walls. Refer to Terminology for more details.

## Habitable area

Total internal floor area of bedrooms, lounge or living rooms, dining rooms, open plan kitchen areas, studies, and entertainment rooms.

## IIC

Impact Insulation Class, degree of soundproofing of the impact noise in a floor or ceiling construction assembly.

## Mixed-use residential development

A development which includes commercial and/or retail areas as well as residential areas within a single building.

## Multi-unit development

Where multiple separate housing units are contained within one building or multiple buildings constructed on a site or complex within a defined boundary.

## MVHR

Mechanical ventilation with heat recovery; an energy recovery ventilation system that extracts stale air from a building while also resupplying fresh filtered air back in.

## NRC

Noise Reduction Coefficient; average sound absorption performance of a material.

## Passive House

Voluntary standard and quality management system designed to achieve ultra-low energy buildings.

## Psi value

Measure of heat loss (“thermal bridging”) within a junction of two thermal elements, measured in W/mK.

## R-value

Thermal resistance rating used to determine a material or assembly’s ability to resist heat flow.

## Room-sealed appliance

An appliance designed so that air for combustion neither enters from, nor combustion products enter into, the room in which the appliance is located. (As defined in NZBC clause G4).

## Rw

Weighted Sound Reduction Index; rating used to measure the level of sound insulation of walls, floors, windows, and doors expressed in decibels (dB). Higher Rw values indicate better sound insulation.

## sDA

Spatial Daylight Autonomy; describes how much of a space receive sufficient daylight.

## Shading

A measure of how much solar heat gain enters through a window compared to an unshaded window.

## Standalone home

A free-standing residential building; detached house with no intertenancy walls.

## STC

Sound Transmission Class; rating of how well a partition in a building attenuates airborne sound.

## Stormwater detention

Systems designed to slow the rate of stormwater runoff.

## Stormwater retention

Systems designed to capture and store stormwater runoff; often re-used to supply non-potable and/or potable water.

## Terraced home

A dwelling with at least one common wall with another dwelling.

## Thermal envelope

The division between the outside (i.e. unconditioned and usually uninsulated) area of the house and the inside (i.e. conditioned and insulated) area of the house.

## Typology

Dwellings with similar size, layout and thermal properties. Refer to Terminology for more detail.

## Volume Certification

Pathway for awarding credit points using a standard specification with a quality assurance process to ensure adherence.

## WELS

Water Efficiency Labelling Scheme; designed to provide information through labelling at the point of sale to consumers buying products that use water.

## Wet room

Rooms that are a common source of moisture in the home: bathrooms, kitchens and laundries.
